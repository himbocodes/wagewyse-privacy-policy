Privacy Policy for WageWyse

Last updated: 20 September 2026

WageWyse is developed by Himbocodes ("we", "us", "our"). If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, contact us at himbocodes@gmail.com.

WageWyse is a rota, pay and budget planning app. It is designed to work without requiring you to create an account or hand over personal information directly to us.

Information We Collect

WageWyse does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location.

All shift, pay and budget information you enter is stored locally on your device. This data is used solely to provide the app's functionality and is never transmitted to Himbocodes.

We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services, and no software development kit (SDK) other than RevenueCat (described below) is integrated into the app.

In-App Purchases

WageWyse uses RevenueCat to process and verify in-app purchases and subscriptions.

When you make or restore a purchase, RevenueCat processes:

  • An anonymous app-user identifier (not linked to your name or email).
  • Purchase and transaction details supplied by the Apple App Store or Google Play (such as product ID, transaction ID and purchase/renewal dates).
  • Basic technical information needed to verify your entitlement to paid features (such as device platform and app version).

This information is used only to verify your purchase and determine access to paid features — never for advertising.

Because purchases are made through Apple's App Store or Google Play, your transaction is also subject to Apple's or Google's own privacy policy, which governs the payment information they hold.

RevenueCat's own privacy practices are described at: revenuecat.com/privacy

Tracking

WageWyse does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and contains no advertising or advertising-tracking services.

Data Sharing

Himbocodes does not sell or rent your personal information.

The only third party WageWyse shares any information with is RevenueCat, for the purchase-verification purpose described above.

Data Stored on Your Device

WageWyse stores your shifts, pay settings, budget data and app preferences locally on your device to provide the app's features.

This data remains on your device unless you delete it through the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. It is not automatically backed up by us, so uninstalling the app or clearing its storage will permanently remove it.

Data Retention and Deletion

Since WageWyse does not require an account and stores data locally, we hold no copy of your app data to retain or delete on our end.

You can delete your locally stored data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Information RevenueCat holds in connection with a purchase (such as transaction records) is retained by RevenueCat under its own retention practices — see its privacy policy linked above, or contact us and we will help direct your request.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over the limited information processed on your behalf by RevenueCat, including the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of that information, and the right to object to its processing. Because RevenueCat may process this information outside your country of residence (including in the United States), transfers are made subject to RevenueCat's own safeguards, described in its privacy policy.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at himbocodes@gmail.com and we will assist or direct you to the appropriate channel.

Children's Privacy

WageWyse is not directed at, and is not intended for use by, children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required by local law in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will address it.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by WageWyse. Locally stored information is subject to the security of your own device. Information transmitted to RevenueCat in connection with purchase verification is handled according to RevenueCat's own security and privacy practices.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated "Last updated" date; where required by law, we will provide additional notice.
